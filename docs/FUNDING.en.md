# Support TimoxVasio

[Français](FUNDING.md) | **English**

TimoxVasio is a free-software project. If the driver, engine, or control application is useful to you, you can help fund their development and maintenance through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/timox).

Funds will primarily be used to buy or rent audio hardware for compatibility testing with hosts and devices, and to rent servers for building, testing, and improving TimoxVasio. They will also help address reported issues, prepare Windows releases, and maintain documentation.

Support is voluntary and does not affect access to the software or its license terms.

The repository Sponsor button depends on enabling GitHub Sponsors for `timox` and the repository's Sponsorships setting. See [GitHub's Sponsor button documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/displaying-a-sponsor-button-in-your-repository).
